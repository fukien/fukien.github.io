About

My name is Wentao Huang (Chinese: 黄文韬). I recently received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the National University of Singapore, where I was advised by Prof. Kian-Lee Tan and collaborated closely with Prof. Bingsheng He. My research focuses on designing high-performance data systems through hardware–software co-design, with particular emphasis on emerging memory technologies (e.g., CXL, HBM), scheduling, and efficient data processing on modern hardware. My broader interests include data management, machine learning systems, high-performance computing, and quantum computing.

I am currently on the industry job market! Please feel free to reach out about research and engineering opportunities.
